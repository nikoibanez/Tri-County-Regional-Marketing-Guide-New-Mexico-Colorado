# Raton Brand System

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

This site uses three distinct visual systems observed on the current public websites for the City
of Raton, Explore Raton, and GrowRaton. The implementation is source-derived and is not a claim of
formal brand approval.

## City of Raton

Source: <https://www.ratonnm.gov/>

- Turquoise: `#21BDC5`
- Deep teal: `#147176`
- Brick: `#772816`
- Deep brown: `#4A221A`
- Sand: `#B3B078`
- Cream: `#EEEDD6`
- Display family observed: `Geogrotesque Slab`
- Body family observed: `Proxima Nova`

The distributed site does not copy proprietary City font files. It requests the observed family
only when already available on a visitor's device, then falls back to the open `Roboto Slab` and
`Inter` web fonts.

## Explore Raton

Sources: <https://www.exploreraton.com/> and <https://www.exploreraton.com/about>

- Turquoise: `#21BDC5`
- Blue: `#2B5672`
- Deep blue: `#22495A`
- Rust: `#775751`
- Orange: `#D97345`
- Pale aqua: `#AAE8EC`
- Brick: `#772816`
- Deep brown: `#4A221A`
- Cream: `#EEEDD6`
- Families observed: `Proxima Nova` and `DIN Next`

Explore Raton colors lead promotion, arts and culture, outward-facing resource routes, navigation
accents, and the primary landing landscape.

## GrowRaton

Source: <https://www.growraton.org/>

- Orange: `#DD8500`
- Deep green: `#043E00`
- Burgundy: `#660001`
- Gray: `#58595B`
- Bright blue: `#00AEEF`
- Families observed: `Arial`, `Helvetica`, `Verdana`, and `Georgia`

GrowRaton colors lead funding, free and discounted tools, templates, and maintenance-oriented
landscapes. Orange is used as an accent rather than small text on a light background; deep green
and burgundy carry contrast-sensitive controls.

## Shared UI Mapping

- Civic and directory surfaces: City of Raton palette
- Promotion and arts surfaces: Explore Raton palette
- Funding, free tools, and growth-support surfaces: GrowRaton palette
- Body text and shared navigation: Explore deep blue on City cream or near-white surfaces
- Focus indicators: City brick, or GrowRaton burgundy on growth-support pages, for strong contrast
- Contextual cursors: City brick arrow plus route-specific City, Explore, or GrowRaton badges

Pale sky and surface tints are derived from the listed source colors by mixing with white. They are
decorative only. Text, links, controls, focus states, and status indicators retain accessible
contrast and do not rely on color alone.
