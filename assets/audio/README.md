# Regional Audio Sources

The Arts & Culture player uses six opt-in field recordings from the Library of Congress collection *Hispano Music and Culture of the Northern Rio Grande: The Juan B. Rael Collection* (AFC 1940/002).

Each Library of Congress item is marked "No known restrictions on use or reproduction." The collection rights page says the Library understands the folk music Juan B. Rael collected in summer 1940 to be within the public domain. The Library also tells users to make their own legal assessment and to consider privacy, publicity, and cultural context.

Rights statement: https://www.loc.gov/collections/hispano-music-and-culture-from-the-northern-rio-grande/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/
