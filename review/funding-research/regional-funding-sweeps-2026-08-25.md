# Regional Funding Sweeps

Research date: 2026-08-25

Geographic focus: Colfax County, New Mexico; Huerfano and Las Animas counties, Colorado; and statewide or national programs that can serve applicants in those counties.

## Review Boundary

The five subjects below were researched as separate passes. Records were added only when an official program or administering-organization page supported a practical funding route. A source appearing in more than one subject is counted in each relevant sweep because its programs serve more than one user need.

Public claims still require human review before merge. Deadlines, eligibility, fiscal-sponsor rules, award amounts, matching requirements, application costs, and marketing-cost eligibility can change.

## Economic Development Sweep

### Added or corrected

- New Mexico LEADS: $5,000-$25,000 cost-reimbursement grants for measurable community economic-development work.
- First Southwest workshop-based business grants: non-repayable awards attached to selected free workshops for rural Colorado entrepreneurs.
- El Pomar competitive grants: rolling operating, program, and capital support for eligible Colorado nonprofits and governments.
- Colorado Strategic Fund Initiatives: limited nonprofit-led regional or key-industry development support.
- Colorado Rural Jump-Start: corrected to reflect that current program support may include operating or new-hire grants in addition to tax benefits.

### Official pages reviewed

- https://edd.newmexico.gov/grants/leads/
- https://www.fswcf.org/grants

### Deferred

- Colorado Rural Ready was not added as a current public record because the available state material did not provide a sufficiently clear current application window and award route.
- Broad economic-development directories were retained as watch sources when they did not identify a specific current non-loan award.

## Automation Expansion Decision

The watch registry now includes 31 official pages. Institutions that yielded multiple useful programs received umbrella or program-specific monitoring coverage:

- Colorado OEDIT: programs hub and Colorado Creates.
- New Mexico Economic Development Department: grants hub, LEADS, and Creative Industries.
- New Mexico Arts: organizational grants and artist opportunities.
- Taos Community Foundation: a single current funding hub covering several western Colfax programs.
- Trinidad State College: one scholarship and grant hub covering several regional student programs.
- El Pomar, Southern Colorado Community Foundation, New Mexico Community Foundation, and the other regional foundations: direct current grant pages.

The weekly report must show separate configured, checked, successful, changed, new-baseline, and failed counts for economic development, education, healthcare, nonprofits, and arts and culture. Automation remains a review queue: it may detect page changes, but it cannot alter public opportunity claims without review.
